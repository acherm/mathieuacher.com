Edit and annotate the site

Every page has a toolbar (bottom right):

  • Edit (key e): click a text, change it in place, Save. When the change maps to one place in the sources, Write to source & rebuild applies it at once; otherwise it stays recorded for later.
  • Comment (key c): click a text, or select a few words, and leave a note (unclear, wrong or outdated, missing, remove).
  • Browse (key b or Esc): normal navigation.
