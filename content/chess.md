I have been playing chess since I was 8. I discovered the game by chance in my home town, joined the Cannes chess club at 11, and had the chance to be trained by strong grandmasters there.

- [FIDE Master](https://ratings.fide.com/profile/618497) with three international master norms (2007, 2008, 2010), FIDE-rated since 1998
- Twice French youth champion (1998, 2002)
- Three World Youth Chess Championships (1998, 1999, 2002) and one European Youth Chess Championship (1998)
- Second place in the French first league with the Cannes chess club (2003)
- Gold medal in the French first league (board 6, 2009)

I no longer play at a high level, but I am always up for a [blitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blitz_chess) game online. Three of [my games](https://www.365chess.com/players/Mathieu_Acher) I like: a [win against Santo-Roman](https://www.365chess.com/view_game.php?g=500307) (French first league, 2000), a [win against Zhigalko](https://www.365chess.com/view_game.php?g=291633) (World Youth Championship, Greece, 2002), and a [draw against Vachier-Lagrave](https://www.365chess.com/view_game.php?g=3473459) (French first league, 2007).

Chess also shows up in my research and teaching:

- How well do large language models play chess? Elo estimates and illegal moves of GPT models against Stockfish ([gptchess](https://github.com/acherm/gptchess), [blog post](https://blog.mathieuacher.com/GPTsChessEloRatingLegalMoves/)), featured in Monsieur Phi's video *ChatGPT rêve-t-il de cavaliers électriques ?* ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6D1XIbkm4JE), in French) and in a talk for school pupils at the Fête de la science 2025 ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TtGiT-tWdmE), in French).
- Chess engines written from scratch by coding agents in 17+ programming languages ([overview](https://blog.mathieuacher.com/FromScratchChessEnginesPolyglot/), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13763)).
- A replication study of metamorphic testing of chess engines ([IST 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.infsof.2025.107679)) and a [large-scale analysis of chess games with Stockfish](https://hal.science/hal-01307091) (5 million games, 270 million positions).
- Object-oriented design and testing courses at INSA Rennes built around the ChessBall project, and discussions about AlphaZero ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PH06mEOVVyY), in French).
