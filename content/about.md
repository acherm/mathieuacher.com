I am a Full Professor of Computer Science at [INSA Rennes](https://www.insa-rennes.fr/), a member (*sociétaire*) of the [Institut Universitaire de France](https://www.iufrance.fr/les-membres-de-liuf/membre/2282-mathieu-acher.html) (IUF), which each year recognizes a small number of French academics for the excellence of their research, and a researcher in the [DiverSE](https://www.diverse-team.fr/) team (Inria, IRISA, CNRS, Université de Rennes). I was appointed junior member of the IUF in 2021. Before joining INSA, I was Associate Professor at the University of Rennes 1 (2012–2022). I co-lead the Inria challenge [LLM4Code](https://project.inria.fr/llm4code/) on reliable code assistants based on large language models, and the Inria–Simula associate team [RIPOST](https://diverse-project.github.io/ripost_ea/) on resilient and reproducible software.

My research is about **variability**: software that comes in many variants and configurations, from the Linux kernel and video encoders to scientific pipelines and machine learning systems. I work on modelling, reverse engineering, testing, and learning these huge configuration spaces, and on what they mean for the reproducibility of science. I also study how large language models and coding agents change the way we build, migrate, and vary software, from legacy COBOL to chess engines written in a dozen programming languages.

I am also a chess player (FIDE Master, three international master norms), always up for a game of blitz.
