I teach software engineering, mostly at [INSA Rennes](https://www.insa-rennes.fr/) in the computer science curriculum. Recent courses:

- **Modelling, verification, advanced programming, and variability** (CPOO1, 3rd year; CPOO2, 4th year): several formalisms to specify software (from class diagrams to feature models), to test it (JUnit, Mockito, mutation testing), and to vary it through time and space. Students apply them in Java, with design patterns, on the ChessBall project (student workspaces: [CPOO1](https://github.com/acherm/cpoo1-etd), [CPOO2](https://github.com/acherm/cpoo2-etd)).
- **Reproducibility of computational experiments** (4th and 5th year, since 2024): a hands-on course going from floating-point non-associativity, Docker, and continuous integration to reproducing and replicating published studies (LLMs playing chess, home advantage in football, energy of programming languages). See the [ACM REP 2025 paper](https://hal.science/hal-05190848) and the [course repository](https://github.com/acherm/REP-INSA2526).
- **Domain-specific languages** (research Master SIF, Rennes, and INSA Rennes since 2025–26): *The Art of Domain-Specific Languages: Let's Hack Our Own Languages!* ([materials](https://github.com/FAMILIAR-project/HackOurLanguages-SIF), [Langium demonstrators](https://github.com/acherm/dsl-langium)). At INSA, students develop DSLs for families of games, including services to play with AI and LLMs.

From 2012 to 2022, I taught at the University of Rennes 1 ([ISTIC](https://istic.univ-rennes.fr/) and ESIR): model-driven engineering, domain-specific languages, software development projects, software design and modelling, and Scala programming, and also at [ENSAI](https://ensai.fr/). I was head of the Master 1 in Software Engineering at ISTIC, coordinator of the Bachelor (L3) internships and projects (2018–2021, 100+ students a year), in charge of teaching services at ISTIC (2014–2017), and a jury member of the *Agrégation d'informatique* (2022).

Beyond the classroom: lectures at the EJCP summer school for PhD students (2018, 2019, and 2023 on reproducible science and software engineering), tutorials at ASE, SPLC, MODELS, and ECOOP/ECSA, and [teaching.variability.io](https://teaching.variability.io/), a shared repository of teaching material on variability and configurable systems (see also the SPLTea workshops).
